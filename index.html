Prologue

The Forgotten Art

The world has slowed. Pages take seconds to load. Servers fall over on ordinary afternoons, and people are leaving the realm for faster lands. The Latency Lord rules, and almost nobody remembers that there was ever a way to fight him.

You are about to learn that there is.

There is also a deadline. Word has arrived of the Surge — a wave of traffic the realm cannot avoid and, as things stand, cannot survive.

One page. Seven parts. Seven different answers.

Every lesson works on the same product page, because the interesting decisions only appear when the same page holds things that disagree about how stale they may be.

The Latency Lord forges his weapons in front of you.

"He does not need to take the cache away. He only needs to choose the moment it empties."
The Forge, Lesson 8

Five times across the course, the villain stops to study something you have just learned, and builds a weapon out of it. By the time the attacks arrive in Lesson 12, you have watched every one of them being made.

So Lesson 12 grants no new technique. It is the only lesson that does not, and that is the point: every defense is something you were handed earlier, in a lesson that never mentioned an attack.
